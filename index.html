Vice-Chancellor's Academic Excellence Scholarship

Offered by: Malawi University of Science and Technology (MUST)

Award: Full tuition coverage for 4 years + book allowance of MWK 250,000 per year.

Who Can Apply: Applicants meeting the academic requirements stated in the official scholarship announcement.

Possible Requirements:

  • Copy of MSCE results
  • Admission letter from MUST
  • Recommendation letter, if required

Deadline: Verify from the latest official announcement.

Higher Education Students' Loans and Grants Board (HESLGB)

Offered by: Government of Malawi

Support: Student loans and grants, depending on the current funding programme and eligibility criteria.

Who Can Apply: Eligible students admitted to recognised higher education institutions who meet the Board's current requirements.

Documents may include:

  • Admission letter
  • Proof of financial circumstances
  • Identification documents

Chevening Scholarship – United Kingdom

Offered by: UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office

Award: Usually includes tuition fees, living allowance, travel and other eligible costs, according to the current award terms.

Who Can Apply: Applicants who meet Chevening's current eligibility criteria, including degree and work-experience requirements.
